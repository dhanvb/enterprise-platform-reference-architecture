**Internal Reference:** A02

## Architectural Question

How should an enterprise select platform technologies without allowing preferred products to dictate architectural requirements?

## Demonstrated Architectural Decisions

1. Architecture requirements precede product selection.
2. Capabilities are separated from vendor implementations.
3. Mandatory constraints take precedence over weighted scoring.
4. CI and GitOps reconciliation are evaluated as distinct capabilities.
5. Operational support and lifecycle costs are part of technology selection.
6. Decision sensitivity must be assessed.
7. Unsupported product scores remain unknown.

## Worked Example

The technology evaluation document includes an illustrative runtime decision using two hypothetical implementation strategies.

It demonstrates that changing evaluation priorities can reverse a ranking.

It does not establish real product superiority.

## What This Publication Does Not Prove

- Production platform deployment
- Vendor benchmarking
- Actual Kubernetes versus OpenShift procurement analysis
- Production security control enforcement
- Achieved SLAs
- Operational recovery performance

These require further implementation or organizational evidence.

## Architectural Conclusion

A defensible enterprise platform technology decision requires traceable requirements, explicit constraints, viable alternatives, documented risks and supporting evidence.

The selection method is established here. Final product selection remains context-dependent.
